Arena — Delete Your Account

App: Arena · Developer: Rahul (cryptork97@gmail.com)

You can ask us to permanently delete your Arena account and the personal data associated with it. Here's how — and see what stays, with your name removed below.

How to delete your account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Arena and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is erased straight away — there is nothing to wait for.

By email

  1. Send an email to cryptork97@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to Arena.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Arena account.
  3. We will verify the request and delete your account, usually within a few days and no later than 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your login account (email and password / Google sign-in).
  • Your profile — display name, email address and avatar.
  • Your votes and any reports you filed.

What stays, with your name removed

Arena is a shared debate app, so deleting one account must not erase other people's conversations. Messages you posted and debate rooms you created are therefore kept, but no longer linked to you — your name and avatar are replaced with "Deleted user", and nothing on them points back to your account. The same applies where someone quoted one of your messages in a reply.

If you want a specific message removed rather than anonymised, delete it in the app before deleting your account, or email us and we will remove it.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you is kept after deletion, except where we are required to retain limited records to comply with the law. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
