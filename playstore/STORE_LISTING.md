## Full description  (max 4000 characters)

Got an opinion? Bring it to the Arena.

Arena is a live debate app for people who actually enjoy a good argument. Jump into a debate room, choose your side — For or Against — and make your case against real people from around the world. No endless feed, no likes to chase. Just honest, head-to-head discussion on the topics people actually care about.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT

🔥 LIVE DEBATE ROOMS
Join any public debate room and start arguing in real time. Every room has one clear topic or question, so the discussion stays sharp and on-point instead of drifting off into noise. Messages appear instantly as people reply, so a good debate feels like a real conversation.

⚖️ PICK YOUR SIDE
Take a stand — For or Against — and your messages get colour-coded so everyone can see exactly where you stand. Prefer to read first? Join as a watcher and follow the argument before you commit to a side.

🏆 VOTE ON WHO WON
Every room has a live "Who won?" vote. Watch opinion swing in real time as the debate heats up and the better arguments land.

🗓️ TOPIC OF THE DAY
A fresh debate topic drops every single day, from serious current events to fun arguments worth having. Get a daily notification, drop in, and give your take before the day is over.

💬 REPLY, REACT, DISCUSS
Swipe any message to reply directly to it, react with emoji, and get a notification the moment someone answers you. It's built for back-and-forth discussion, not one-way posting.

🔒 PRIVATE DEBATE ROOMS
Want to debate just your friends, your class, or your team? Create a private room with a password and keep the discussion to your circle. Share a room in one tap.

🛡️ REAL PEOPLE, MODERATED
Every account is a verified, real person, so you're debating humans and not bots. Report, block, and moderation tools keep discussions civil even when opinions get heated.

WHAT CAN YOU DEBATE?

Anything worth arguing about. Science, religion, politics, movies, sport, technology, history — plus whatever the community starts today. Is AI good for society? Is the greatest of all time really the greatest? Should the rules change? If people disagree about it, there's a room for it — and if there isn't, you can start one in seconds.

WHAT MAKES ARENA DIFFERENT

Most social apps reward you for agreeing. Arena rewards you for thinking.

Comment sections bury the good arguments. Group chats wander off topic. Forums are slow. Arena keeps one topic, two sides, and a live room of people willing to defend their view. It's a debate club, a discussion forum, and a live chat room in one — built for people who like to defend a point, and who actually want to hear the other side.

GREAT FOR

• Anyone who loves to debate and discuss current events
• Students and debate club members practising their arguments
• Friends who want a private room to settle an argument properly
• Anyone tired of shouting into a feed and wanting a real discussion

Pick your side. Make your point. Win the room.

Download Arena and join the debate.

## ASO maintenance (the report's third recommendation)

Revisit this listing every couple of months:
1. Check which search terms actually bring installs in Play Console →
   Grow users → Store performance → **Store listing acquisition**.
2. Work the winning terms into the short description and the first two lines of
   the full description (Play weights early text more heavily).
3. Refresh the "What can you debate?" examples with topics that are trending —
   it keeps the listing current and gives you a reason to update.
